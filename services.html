Clear Workflow

Four Steps To Smooth Handover

A transparent execution process designed to avoid site delays and keep your facility team informed at every stage.

Step 01 • Site Check

Measure Space And Water Needs

We review your available footprint, plinth readiness, inlet/outlet levels, and daily capacity so every component arrives ready to fit.

Step 02 • Clean Erection

Assemble Without Heavy Civil Work

Factory-punched panels and liners are assembled cleanly on site by trained technicians with minimal noise or disruption.

Step 03 • Quality Check

Run Full Hydro-Test Before Use

Every tank and treatment skid undergoes complete water-fill testing and pressure checks to confirm 100% leak-free operation.

Keep Performance Peak Year-Round

Routine preventive visits, sanitization, and spare-part readiness protect water purity and extend equipment life.
