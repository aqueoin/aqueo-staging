Who Are We

A Complete Water Solutions Partner

Founded in 2014 as an arm of Pure Aquatech Solutions, Aqueo brings over a decade of expertise in water solutions across industrial, commercial, and residential sectors.

What We Do

We supply bolted Zincalume tanks, sectional SMC-FRP panel tanks, MS-FRP vessels, WTP/STP/RO systems, and repair & maintenance services.

Who We Serve

Factories, logistics hubs, commercial buildings, hospitals, hotels, townships, and community water projects needing fast, leak-free storage.

How We Deliver

Pre-engineered modular components bolt together cleanly on site—reducing civil delays, avoiding welding hassles, and supporting long-term upkeep.
