Zero-Demolition Upgrade

Leak-Proof Tank Liner 

Aging concrete (RCC) or corroded steel tanks often develop hairline cracks, seepage, and hygiene issues. Instead of costly civil demolition, Aqueo installs custom-fabricated reinforced liners inside your existing structure.

  • Fast Turnaround: Installed inside existing sumps or overhead tanks in days with minimal downtime.
  • Total Seepage Barrier: Food-grade and industrial-grade UV-stabilized membranes isolate water from degraded walls.
  • Extended Asset Life: Restores watertight integrity and extends service life by 15+ years.
