Industrial Process & Raw Water

CSR & Community Drinking Water

2. Water Treatment & Softening (WTP)

Source water from borewells and municipal lines often carries high hardness, turbidity, iron, or suspended solids that damage boilers, chillers, and plumbing networks.

  • Multi-grade sand & activated carbon filtration
  • Automatic & manual cation-resin water softeners
  • Iron removal & pre-treatment skids

3. Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO)

High-recovery RO systems engineered to remove dissolved salts (TDS), heavy metals, and pathogens for manufacturing processes and institutional potable supply.

  • 500 LPH to 25,000+ LPH skid-mounted configurations
  • Low-fouling membranes & automated CIP flushing
  • Integrated UV/Ozonation post-treatment

4. Wastewater Treatment (STP)

Compact, odor-controlled sewage treatment plants designed to meet CPCB/SPCB discharge norms for industrial estates, commercial buildings, hotels, and hospitals.

  • MBBR & SBR biological treatment technologies
  • Prefabricated modular & containerized footprints
  • Low power consumption & simplified operator controls

5. Tertiary Treatment & Water Reuse

Convert treated wastewater into a dependable non-potable resource—cutting fresh tanker dependency and supporting corporate ESG/zero-discharge goals.

  • Ultrafiltration (UF) & tertiary polishing units
  • Safe reuse for HVAC cooling towers, toilet flushing & horticulture
  • Integrated treated-water storage & hydro-pneumatic distribution
